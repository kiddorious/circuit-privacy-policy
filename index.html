Privacy Policy for Circuit

Last updated: 23 September 2026

The short version: Circuit does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. Your progress is saved on your own device. We have no servers, no accounts and no way to identify you.

What we collect

Nothing. Circuit has no sign-in, no profile, no analytics account tied to you, and no server of our own that your device talks to.

What is stored on your device

The game saves the following locally, so that closing the app does not lose your progress:

  • Which levels you have completed, and your best times
  • Your daily-puzzle history and streak
  • Settings such as language, sound and board style
  • Whether you have purchased the ad-free upgrade

This data never leaves your device unless you deliberately copy your transfer code and move it to another device yourself. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Advertising

The free version of Circuit shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use data to serve those ads, including your device's advertising identifier. We do not receive that data, and we cannot link it to you.

Google's own privacy policy explains what they collect and why:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device. Purchasing the ad-free upgrade stops the app requesting ads at all.

Purchases

The optional "remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details — the app only receives a yes/no answer about whether the purchase exists.

Children

Circuit is suitable for all ages. It contains no chat, no user-generated content, no social features and no links to other users. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, because we do not collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. To remove everything Circuit has stored, either use Settings → Reset all progress inside the game, or uninstall the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top.
